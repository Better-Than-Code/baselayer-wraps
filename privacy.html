PRIVACY POLICY

BaseLayer Wraps • Jacksonville, Florida • Effective Date: October 2026

BaseLayer Wraps ("Company," "we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

When you register on our intake platform, we collect:

  • Contact Information: Your name (or display name/anonymity preference) and email address.
  • Vehicle & Specification Data: Your ride type (e.g., e-bike battery or scooter stem) and preferred colorway/finish.
  • Transaction Metadata: Order identifiers, support amounts, and payment status logs securely handled via Square.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data strictly for internal operational purposes:

  • Processing your pre-order, appointment scheduling queue, or Founder Wall recognition status.
  • Dispatching transactional email confirmations, status updates, and launch announcements.
  • Maintaining our internal secure ledger for shop fulfillment.

3. Data Sovereignty & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third-party advertisers. Financial transactions are securely processed externally via Square. Lead intake data is stored within our sovereign, privately controlled Google Sheets ledger.

4. Public Founder Wall Display

If you back our shop via a paid tier, your display name (or designated initials) and backing tier badge will appear on our public Founder Wall unless you explicitly toggle the "Display as Anonymous" privacy option during registration.

5. Contact

To request data access, corrections, or deletion, please reply directly to any confirmation email or contact us at support@baselayerwraps.com.
