TERMS OF PRE-ORDER

BaseLayer Wraps • Jacksonville, Florida • Effective Date: October 2026

These Terms of Pre-Order ("Terms") govern the placement of pre-orders and foundational backing for custom vehicle wraps provided by BaseLayer Wraps ("Company," "we," "our," or "us"). By submitting a pre-order or backing contribution, you agree to be bound by these Terms.

1. Pre-Order & Deposit Structure

Placing a Rider Pre-Order ($25) secures your position in our priority appointment queue and includes introductory perks as outlined on our intake registration interface. The deposit represents partial payment toward the total retail cost of your scheduled installation, with the remaining balance due upon shop check-in and prior to final vehicle release.

2. Refund & Cancellation Policy

100% Refundable Deposits: Pre-order and deposit funds are fully refundable at any time prior to your official appointment booking confirmation. Once your appointment date is formally scheduled and materials are custom-allocated, deposits transition to non-refundable service commitments. To request a refund or modify your reservation at any time prior to scheduling, simply reply directly to your confirmation email.

3. Community Founder Backing

Contributions made through the Community Founder tier represent direct financial backing for shop tooling and operational buildout. Founder tier contributions are non-refundable financial supports that secure your permanent placement and rank badge on our public Founder Wall.

4. Scheduling & Execution

Launch timelines and appointment windows are estimated based on material arrival and shop capacity. Priority booking access ensures pre-order holders receive calendar selection notices before our intake schedule opens to the general public.

5. Contact

For all inquiries, modifications, or refund requests, please contact our team directly by replying to your transactional confirmation email or emailing support@baselayerwraps.com.
